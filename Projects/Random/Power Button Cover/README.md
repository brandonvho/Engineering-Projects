# Power Button Cover

## Overview
My brother's cat would sit on his computer and accidentally press the power button, turning it off. He connects remotely to his computer from his laptop so he isn't able to turn it back on when he is away. He asked me for a solution.

## Objectives
- Create a cover that will sit over the power button to prevent turning on or off.
- It should not be easily removed.
- It should look minimalistic and not stand out.

## Requirements & Constraints
- Color match the computer.
- Mounting without glue or tape.

## Design Summary
- Circular shape that is larger than the power button to use the computer's case to support the cover. Long rectangle protruding from the circle and prongs sticking down into the mic and headphone jack to use as friction fit. Printed in white PLA to match the computer's case.

## Tools & Technologies
- CAD: Fusion
- Manufacturing: Bambu Lab P1S

## Testing & Results
- After printing, I test-fit the cover by hand over the power button and jacks. The prongs held the cover securely in place via friction fit, with no glue or tape needed. I applied pressure by hand to simulate the cat sitting on the cover, and it did not shift or allow the button underneath to be pressed. The part fit as designed on the first print, with no complications.

## Challenges & Lessons Learned
- The design and print process went smoothly overall, with no major issues. The dimensions measured for the power button and jack spacing translated well from CAD to the final print, resulting in an accurate friction fit on the first attempt.

## Future Improvements
- If tighter fit is needed or if the reset button is an issue, then I will redesign.
