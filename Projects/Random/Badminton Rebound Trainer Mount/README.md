# Badminton Rebound Trainer Mount

## Overview
My friend asked me if I could create something to mount his Badminton Rebound Trainer.

## Objectives
- Create a mount that holds the trainer in place, allowing it to be mounted on the ceiling with either double-sided tape or wood screws.

## Requirements & Constraints
- Holds the trainer in place without breaking off or falling off during use.
- Provide a surface large enough for double-sided tape, with counterbored screw holes for mounting into ceiling wood.

## Design Summary
- Square mounting bracket with a slot that narrows to tighten the grip on the tabs of the Badminton Rebound Trainer.

## Tools & Technologies
- CAD: SolidWorks
- Manufacturing: Bambu Lab P1S (PLA)

## Testing & Results
- Not yet tested.

## Challenges & Lessons Learned
- I positioned the screw holes in-line with the center for mounting into the wood, spaced far enough apart to avoid being covered by the Rebound Trainer, allowing for easy installation and removal.
- My caliper wasn't thin enough to measure inside the Rebound Trainer's mounting structure (a 2mm gap), so I had to estimate the gap by eye as closely as possible.

## Future Improvements
- My friend will use the Rebound Trainer and let me know if there are any issues with strength or sizing.
