# Toy Design 
 
## Overview 
Articulated 3D-Printed Toy Design Challenge for Intro to Engineering
 
## Objectives 
- Toy must be safe, durable, and intuitive to interact with.
- Designed for a child between 2-4 years old.
- Design must have a moving part (articulate).
 
## Requirements & Constraints 
- Must include at least one articulated mechanism.
- No sharp edges.
- No small detachable parts that could pose a choking hazard.

## Design Summary 
My design theme is construction vehicles. My toy design will have 7 total parts, a base with moving wheels, 3 front pieces, and 3 back pieces. The 3 front pieces are a driver cabin, a plower, and a roller. The 3 back pieces are a mixer, a dumper, and an engine. All moving parts will be print in place joints and axles. All pieces will have magnets that are glued and pushed into place to become interchangable.
 
## Tools & Technologies 
- CAD: Fusion
- Manufacturing: Bambu Lab P1S
- Software: Bambu Studio
 
## Testing & Results 
There was times when the clearance for the print in place joints were too small and the filament welded together. I could have changed the settings to be slower and finer layers. 
I used Bambu studio's built in support structures. 
 
## Future Improvements 
I want to try to get the clearance for the print in place joints to be as small as possible.
